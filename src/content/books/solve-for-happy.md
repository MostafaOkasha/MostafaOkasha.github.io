## The framework

**6 Grand Illusions** — Thought · Self · Knowledge · Time · Control · Fear

**7 Blind Spots** — Filters · Assumptions · Predictions · Memories · Labels · Emotions · Exaggeration

**5 Ultimate Truths** — Now · Change · Love · Death · Design

The core equation: **Happiness ≥ Your perception of the events of your life − Your expectations of how life should behave.** Bust the six illusions that keep you in confusion, fix the seven blind spots that distort your judgment, and hold on to the five ultimate truths.
